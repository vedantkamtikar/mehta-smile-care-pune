# Mehta Smile Care Pune

Build a responsive, mobile-first website for Dr. Mehta's Dental Care & Cure, a multispeciality dental and orthodontic clinic in Baner-Pashan Link Road, Pune. Doctors: Dr. Kunal Mehta and Dr. Ripple Mehta.

Positioning: a calm, gentle, spotless clinic for people who are nervous about dentists, including anxious adults and parents of children getting braces. Tone: warm, reassuring, professional. No hype or superlatives like "best" or "#1".

Design:

- Palette: deep navy (#2C5F8F) as primary, coral (#E8604C) for CTAs and accents, white and soft off-white backgrounds.

- Fonts: Poppins for headings, Inter for body.

- Rounded corners, generous whitespace, soft shadows, calm and clean feel.

- Define colors and fonts as theme tokens so they're easy to change.

Navigation: sticky header with logo, links (About, Treatments, Gallery, Reviews, Contact) and a coral "Book Appointment" button. On mobile, use a hamburger menu plus a sticky bottom bar with three buttons: Call, WhatsApp, Book.

Single-page layout with these sections in order: Hero, Trust strip, Services, Meet the Doctors, Reassurance ("Nervous about the dentist?"), Reviews, Gallery, Visit Us, Footer.

For now use clear placeholder text and placeholder images in every section. I will upload the logo and photos next. Do not invent any facts, prices, or credentials.
